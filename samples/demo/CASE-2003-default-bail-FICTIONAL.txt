IN THE COURT OF THE SESSIONS JUDGE, EXAMPLE DISTRICT (FICTIONAL)
CRIMINAL MISCELLANEOUS (BAIL) APPLICATION No. 2003 of 2023
The applicant (accused No. 2) versus The State of Example State

1. The applicant was arrested on 2 January 2023 in connection with an FIR registered at Example Nagar Police Station for
   offences of criminal conspiracy and cheating, and has been in judicial custody since 3 January 2023.
2. The period of 90 days for completing the investigation expired on 2 April 2023. No charge sheet, that is no final report
   under Section 173 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, was filed within that period, and the prosecution did not apply for
   any extension of time.
3. On 3 April 2023 the applicant applied for default bail under the proviso to Section 167(2) of the Code of Criminal
   Procedure. The investigating agency filed a charge sheet on 5 April 2023, after the application had been made, and opposed
   bail on the ground that the charge sheet had since been filed.
4. The applicant submits that his indefeasible right to statutory bail accrued when the 90 days expired and was availed of when
   he applied, before any charge sheet was filed. Filing the charge sheet afterwards cannot defeat that right, and the time
   spent in custody cannot be extended by keeping the application pending.
5. He is ready to furnish bail bonds and to comply with any conditions the Court imposes.

RELIEF
Release the applicant on default bail under Section 167(2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure on such terms as the Court thinks fit.
